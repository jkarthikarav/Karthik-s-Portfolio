Visual Design for Print, Web, and Digital

Introduction

Since February 2020, I have collaborated with various SMB and Start-up/Scale-up clients to handle various aspects of branding: naming, logo, corporate identity, catalogs, brochures, and monographs, packaging, displays, events, and BtL (Below the Line) materials, as well as video, web, and digital design, covering all of their visual communication needs. Although for a majority of my clients, I was involved in a pivotal point in their histories.

Many of the people and businesses who I worked with had little or no online presence. Some were long standing family run brands. Others were newer companies still figuring out their digital identity. Some just wanted specific material to support their ideas. What united them was the same challenge: Quality products, Non-Existant Digital visibility.

For example, one client, “Her Choice Fashions,” had been around since 1982 but had never properly used digital media to their benefit. Their competitors sold lower quality goods and had an appalling in-person Customer Experience, but they outperformed my client simply through being active online.

So, What did I do?

I made posters using Adobe, graphic design posts, yes for that is the layman's term to describe what I did on the outside. But on the inside, I was creating sustainable information systems, content directions, and stylized concepts.
