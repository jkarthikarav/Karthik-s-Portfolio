Visual Design

Focus: Brand, Marketing, and Digital Design.

Scope: End-to-end visual execution across print, web, social, and video-led platforms.

Since February 2020, I have collaborated with SMB and start-up/scale-up clients on branding and visual communication, including logos, corporate identity, catalogs, brochures, packaging, displays, BtL materials, and digital media such as video and web design. Deliverables also include brand guidelines, web and mobile UI assets, email templates, presentation decks, and infographics.

I have also worked on projects for movies and OTT/TV shows.

Movies: The Akaali

Working at Fix It In Post Studios, I was given the opportunity to work as an assistant Editor on the movie "The Akaali" and design the first look posters. I composited the 3D models our 3D team made to create this first look poster. The other first look poster I worked on is used as the image for the banner of this page.

Streaming Show: Oh My Kadhali (Transl. Oh My Lover)

Adbee Films was making a comeback during the Pandemic with their direct-to-streaming show. I made thumbnails for the 7-episodes + Trailer as well as promotional posters.
