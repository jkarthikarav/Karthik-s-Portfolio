  <h3 class="casestudy-body-headline" style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-default);">
    Specific sections of our New IA
  </h3>

  <div class="casestudy-tabs">
    <div class="tab-buttons">
    <button class="tab-btn active" data-tab="plan-visit">Plan Your Visit</button>
    <button class="tab-btn" data-tab="explore">Explore</button>
  </div>
  <hr>

  <div class="tab-content active" id="plan-visit">
    <h4 style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-default);">Plan Your Visit</h4>
    <p class="casestudy-body-text" style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-muted); margin-bottom: 20px;">
      We restructured the IA sub-tree for "Plan Your Visit" to provide direct paths to revenue actions: 
      Admissions and Book a Group Tour. The existing site relied on a single "Visit" page with an in-page anchor menu. This forced users to scroll through eight sections:
      Admissions, Holiday Hours, Upcoming Programming, Members, Getting Here, Community Access, Accessibility, and Tour, to find the information they needed. 
      The sub-level content shows the items users were most likely trying to access.
    </p>

    <p class="caption-text" style="font-size: small; margin: 1rem 0; color: var(--jka-color-text-inv-default);">Pictured below: Plan Your Visit Section</p>
    <img src="../../assets/images/webp/tmc-ia-1.webp" class="user-journey-image-mrs" 
    style="width:50%; cursor:pointer; background-color: var(--jka-color-surface-default);" 
    onclick="window.open('../../assets/images/webp/tmc-ia-1.webp','_blank')">
    
    <p class="casestudy-body-text" style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-muted); margin-bottom: 20px;">
      The new structure elevates Admissions and Book a Group Tour to primary sub-levels and links directly to the corresponding conversion forms. 
      This supports the stakeholders’ goal of making logistical information easily accessible, particularly for older audiences, 
      by replacing long, anchor-link pages with a clear, shallow hierarchy that separates logistics from exploration.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="tab-content" id="explore">
    <h4 style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-default);">Explore</h4>
    <p class="casestudy-body-text" style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-muted); margin-bottom: 20px;">
      We restructured the IA sub-tree for "Explore" to provide direct paths to "Current," "Upcoming," and "Past" exhibitions and events. 
      The existing site relied on a single "What's On" menu item, forcing users to filter through paginated months and tags on one page to find specific content. 
      The sub-level content shows the items users might be looking at.
    </p>

    <p class="caption-text" style="font-size: small; margin: 1rem 0; color: var(--jka-color-text-inv-default);">Pictured below: Explore Section</p>
    <img src="../../assets/images/webp/tmc-ia-2.webp" class="user-journey-image-mrs" 
    style="width:50%; cursor:pointer; background-color: var(--jka-color-surface-default);"
    onclick="window.open('../../assets/images/webp/tmc-ia-2.webp','_blank')">
    
    <p class="casestudy-body-text" style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-muted); margin-bottom: 20px;">
      The new structure allows each menu item to link directly to a pre-filtered results page, supporting the stakeholders' goal of faster event discovery and booking. 
      By building the IA around the museum's existing tag and filter logic, the navigation can automatically update as new exhibits are added. 
      This reduces technical maintenance and allows the team to focus on content creation rather than navigation upkeep.
    </p>

    <p class="casestudy-body-text" style="color: var(--jka-color-surface-muted); margin-bottom: 20px;">
      This design satisfies the museum's requirement for better archival visibility by providing a dedicated home for Past Events, making them "easily accessible" without the need for manual filtering.
    </p>
  </div>
