The Karthik Story

I've been designing since 2019. Not doing coffee runs or watching training videos; I've been doing real work, for real organisations, with real impact.

That's been the standard I've held myself to from the start, and it hasn't changed.

My first project was a voluntary engagement with UNICEF India in 2019, where I was recommended by two of my professors at Anna University, Chennai. I produced the wireframes and high-fidelity UI screens that shaped the core user flow of an app built to give activists direct access to legal resources.

Around the same time, Caterpillar's Chennai office brought me in as a two-week trainee to run a guerrilla UX research project.

I delivered usable data and actionable insights at the final presentation; resulting in an extension of my traineeship, and I spent the following weeks shadowing senior designers and sitting in on Agile sprints. I've always been drawn to the problems and my instincts showed up early.

2016-2021: My First Master's Degree and Professional Roots

I was in a combined Bachelor's and Master's program in Media at Anna University, Chennai - considered one of the top public universities in India.

Event Photo. Hosting the 3-Day Documentary screening and panel discussion section of the India International Science Film Festival 2017.

I graduated from Anna University in June 2021 with a five-year Integrated Master's in Electronic Media with a thesis on the Release the #Snyder Cut digital fandom movement through the lens of Optimal Distinctiveness Theory.

The unconventional ideas and the extremely granular research were always me. Niche is where I've always operated best.

I'd already been helping FIIP Studios pro bono with legal and compliance work before I joined; because I believed in what they were building and the people building it.

Once in, I moved quickly into leadership, taking on only the most niche and creatively demanding projects the studio handled.

2021 - 2023: JKR Business Consultancy

I graduated in June 2021, by September I had co-founded a Business Development-focused Firm.

The consultancy was my vehicle for a deliberately broad practice: fintech, education, industrial manufacturing, marketing. Each engagement was chosen for its complexity and the opportunity to design solutions that didn't yet have a template.

Alongside JKR, I've maintained a freelance practice running continuously since 2019.

I don't take on all freelance projects, when my primary commitments are running at full capacity, I'm selective. The projects that make it through are the ones that are intellectually demanding and genuinely interesting.

2023-2025: Toronto & the iSchool

In September 2023, I moved to Toronto to pursue a Master of Information (UX) at the University of Toronto's Faculty of Information aka the iSchool.

Graduation PhotoBooth Picture. Pictured Above (From L-R, Tp-Bt): Kyle, Jay, Alexis, Ashfaaq, Quinn, Karthik, and Nicholas.

I chose the programme because Information, as a discipline, is core to how I think; not because I wanted a degree with UX in the title.

I specialised in UX Design, but deliberately built breadth into my studies: Data Analytics with Python and R, Culture and Technology for theoretical and philosophical grounding, Library Sciences and Information Behaviour, and a cross-faculty elective at the Faculty of Music where I spent a semester developing a business model for a music business.

Even as a student, my need to work didn't stop. I had paused my work at JKR, but I was still freelancing. I worked on a GenAI-assisted pitch deck for a Director presenting to Netflix India.

Meanwhile, Uoft's ECE department brought me on to maintain their website and develop multiple core pages such as industry opportunities, news, the homepage, and a full migration of the previously standalone TNFC lab into their main site.
