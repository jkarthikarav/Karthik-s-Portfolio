2018-2020. Honed by the Winds of Winter.

In Winter 2018, I decided to purchase Adobe CC while recovering from a broken knee.

As a teenager in India, freelancing is dangerous: from getting scammed by multiple bad actors to being shortchanged by organizations due to being ethnically Indian, I've faced it all. I focused on honing my skills and building up my foundations while freelancing and working pro bono.

I was between my third and fourth year of my 5-year degree in Media. Everyone wanted to make short films and work in showbiz, I wanted to solve problems.

My first project was a volunteer role with UNICEF India in Summer 2019, where I was recommended by two of my professors at Anna University, Chennai. I produced high-fidelity UI screens that shaped the core user flow of an app built to give activists direct access to legal resources.

In the Fall, I interviewed with Caterpillar who brought me in as a two-week trainee. I was asked to do a UX research project, typically asked of interns hired at busy companies.

I presented my data and insights on the final day; resulting in an extension of my traineeship, and I spent the following weeks shadowing senior designers and sitting in on Agile sprints.

2020-21. Master's Degree and Professional Ventures.

By my fifth/final year (2020-21), I joined FIIP Studios, and took on the most niche and creatively demanding projects the studio handled.

Event Photo. Hosting the 3-Day Documentary screening and panel discussion section of the India International Science Film Festival 2017.

I graduated in June 2021 with a five-year Integrated Master's in Electronic Media from Anna University, Chennai, considered one of the top public universities in India.

I also wrote a thesis on the Release the #Snyder Cut digital fandom movement.

Even though I had graduated in June 2021, by September I had co-founded a Business Development-focused Firm, JKR Business Consultancy.

Alongside JKR, I've continued my freelance practice that I'd been doing since 2018. When my primary commitments are at full capacity, I only take on the intellectually demanding and genuinely interesting freelance projects.

2023-2025. Toronto & the iSchool.

In September 2023, I moved to Toronto to pursue a Master of Information (UX) at the University of Toronto's Faculty of Information aka the iSchool.

Graduation PhotoBooth Picture. Pictured Above (From L-R, Tp-Bt): Kyle, Jay, Alexis, Ashfaaq, Quinn, Karthik, and Nicholas.

I chose the programme because Information, as a discipline, is core to how I think; not because I wanted a degree with UX in the title.

I specialised in UX Design, but deliberately built breadth: Data Analytics with Python and R Studio, Theoretical and philosophical courses, and Library Sciences and Information Behaviour, as well as a cross-faculty elective at the Faculty of Music where I spent a semester developing a business model for a music-focused AI business.

Even as a student, my need to work didn't stop. I had paused my work at JKR, but I was still freelancing. I worked on a GenAI-assisted pitch deck for a Director presenting to Netflix India.

While I was in my second year of my Master of Information, U of T's ECE department brought me on to maintain their website.

2025 till Now

After the iSchool, I joined Copywriting Creatives, quickly settling into the core leadership team.
