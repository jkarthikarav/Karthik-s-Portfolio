UI Design Showcase: NFC Payment App Screens

    November 8, 2025 • UI Design, Figma, Mobile Design, UX, Fintech

Clearloop Pay is a concept mobile app built to show how employees can manage company expenses. My Brother previously worked in the employee expense department and his work in assessing the bills and reimbursements requests seemed interesting enough for me to think of a way to make that process simpler for him.

An employee is given a company card for expenses, true in many parts of the world. But they are for different levels of employees and different purposes. But in this day and age, NFC is very common -- so is pre-paying for everything digitally. But are there any expenses which cannot be done online AND needs to be a physical card tap?

Design Planning

I shortlisted these down to Meals and Dining expenses, Gas, and Parking ticket payments. Restaurant dining bill amounts, unlike Doordash orders, aren't known beforehand and you have to pay for them phsyically. Gas at the gas station is the same, and unlike Uber/Lyft, we don't all know how much the gas for the trip is going to cost us. And for when you travel on company business, you'll surely have to park somewhere where parking can be paid with a physical tap.

Now, I know that for Parking, you get a receipt after you pay as it depends on how long you park. But for restaurant dining and gas, you can see the amount before you pay. Technically there are some minor exceptions, some parking lots do charge flat rates, or if its a catered event at a restaurant, etc, but we are looking at the broader and more recurring categories.

Based on all of this thinking, I designed the UI for the app using Material Design and a minimal color palette. It is displayed using the Restaurant dining expense flow.

My UI Designs

The screens are:
1. Home/Spend - Dashboard with recent transactions, summaries, and statuses, as well an option to add a new transaction.
2. Photo screen and confirmation – to capture bill image, and to retake/upload.
3. Verification screen - OCR extracts details such as vendor, date, and amount for confirmation or editing; option to subit to HR with the bill image.
4. NFC screens - allows employee to acesss the Company's NFC-enabled credit card to complete payment.
