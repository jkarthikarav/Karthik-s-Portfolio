Designing Components

The most important component that had to be designed was the list items component one would see on the main dashboard/home page.

Other components such as primary and secondary buttons, photo capture-related buttons, text inputs, and banner messaging were created.

High Fidelity UI Screens

1. Home/Spend - Dashboard with recent transactions, summaries, and statuses, as well an option to add a new transaction.

2. Photo screen and confirmation – to capture bill image, and to retake/upload.

3. Verification screen - OCR extracts details such as vendor, date, and amount for confirmation or editing; option to subit to HR with the bill image.

4. NFC screens - allows employee to acesss the Company's NFC-enabled credit card to complete payment.
