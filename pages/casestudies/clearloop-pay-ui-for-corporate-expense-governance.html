Is there Friction in the Corporate Expense Loop?

Managing work-related expenses has two major friction points for both employees and HR: security risk from physical corporate cards and rejection of requests due to receipt attachments being blurry/forgotten. Employees might delay submitting receipts, leading to audit risks and significant lag for HR.

I propose an app: Clearloop Pay, a native mobile application designed to address this gap by changing it into a secure, single-app loop. The design strategy was to simplify the employee's workflow while providing the company with control. Users can:

Designing Components

The interface was designed around managing status and actions. The main dashboard component was prioritized to give the employee immediate information on recent transactions & reimbursement statuses.

Other essential components, such as primary and secondary buttons, photo captures, and text inputs were developed to maintain consistency across the user journey.

NFC Payment Flow

The NFC feature is the core feature of Clearloop Pay. By replacing physical cards with a digital, NFC-enabled virtual card, companies gain remote control. They can instantly issue access and revoke without the hassle or risk associated with lost physical cards.

These screens below provides the tap-to-pay interface for authorized business expenses. The transaction status is immediately reflected in the app's main dashboard on the Spend screen.

Enforcing Documentation at Point of Purchase

1. Home/Spend screen: Dashboard provides summary of recent transactions, status updates, and a CTA for making new transactions.

2. Photo screens: I designed it to require immediate capture of the receipt before the payment (for restaurant expenses), crucial to eliminate errors (like forgotten attachments) and ensure the option to pay is enabled.

3. Verification screen: OCR extracts details (vendor, date, amount) for confirmation or editing. By requiring confirmation before submission, we reduce delays for HR, and reduce any instances of invoice fraud.
