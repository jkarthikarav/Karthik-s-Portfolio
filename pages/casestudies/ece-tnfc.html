What did I achieve with this migration?

This migration required building with scalability in mind. The pages I proposed were structured using reusable components and patterns that could be extracted and applied beyond TNFC.
The impact of that architectural & UI standardisation work became clear after my departure from the organization (This was my last project for ECE). Development on the rest of the site, as well as newly created page Grid Modernization Centre, reuse elements and patterns that were originally designed for TNFC's section.

What I Learned

This was not just a website design. This was about designing content and unifying digital systems to reduce administrative work and signal a cohesive brand identity.
Every design decision was driven by compliance and regulation, including access, training, billing, and safety. At the same time, the pages were designed to feel native to ECE while clearly articulating TNFC’s value. The result positions TNFC as a core, high-value research facility within the department.
