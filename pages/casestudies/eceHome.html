ECE Homepage Redesign

Turning an academic homepage into a student recruitment portal

March 2025 • Role: UX/UI and Web Developer • Work for: ECE Department at the University of Toronto • Mockups + WordPress Development using Figma, WordPress

The Electrical & Computer Engineering (ECE) department at U of T needed to transform their academic homepage into a recruitment tool for prospective students, Departmental identity and student-focused storytelling had to be balanced while not looking/feeling outdated.

Goals

1. Reimagine the homepage as a digital ambassador to attract prospective students
2. Improve web content, navigation, and access to key info
3. Align and Ensure visual design and layout with Faculty of Applied Science & Engineering (FASE) web visual standards as well as the University of Toronto brand guidelines
4. Support a range of stakeholder needs; from students to faculty and industry partners

Key Challenges

Unimpactful Hero Section

Legacy State: Hero section lacked student-focused visuals
Why it matters: Weak first impression for prospective students

Cluttered Navigation

Legacy State: Navigation had too many unorganized links
Why it matters: Users struggled to find key programs and events

Outdated Program Content

Legacy State: Program and event content was outdated
Why it matters: Students could not quickly understand offerings

Research and Co-Op Visibility

Legacy State: Opportunities were buried or unclear
Why it matters: Students and partners could not see key opportunities

Design Process

1. Content Audit & Discovery Research

Assessed other FASE faculty sites and leading global engineering department portals

Mapped gaps: missing CTAs, unclear section hierarchy, outdated hero imagery

2. Synthesizing Insights, Ideating Elements, Building Wireframes

Built wireframes in Figma to clarify information layout

Visual hierarchy focused on what prospective students searched for first

3. Stakeholder Presentations & Refining Draft Builds

Presented concepts to department leadership

Iterated based on feedback from academic admin, researchers, and staff

4. Complete Build on WordPress to LIVE

Handled front-end implementation, ensuring responsive and modular section design

Deployed content and built reusable components for future campaigns

What I Learned

The ECE Homepage Redesign turned a dense academic site into a clear, student‑focused recruitment tool. Guided by research, competitive benchmarking, and stakeholder input, the final design improves navigation, aligns with faculty branding, and highlights programs and events in just a few scrolls. The result is a responsive, modular homepage that strengthens trust, reflects departmental excellence, and supports future recruitment efforts.

Key Takeaway: Every page has a journey

A page is not just content. It has to be a narrative that guides users, shaping what they see, understand, and do.
