What did I learn from studying the Website?

Content Issues. The page makes users work too hard to understand Selenium’s value.

Layout Issues. The homepage is dominated by a heavy wall of content.

Accessibility and Focus. The brand color is used everywhere as backgrounds, headings, links, and buttons across multiple surface colors, so nothing stands out.
