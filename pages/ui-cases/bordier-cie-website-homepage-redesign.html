Private Banking Sector

What does the Website's Homepage convey to its Visitors?

The hero section focuses on a broad reputation claim: “The world’s leading Swiss private banker”. The follow-up paragraph about heritage and trust adds more narrative through “For 5 generations the Bordier family has been preserving and growing wealth…”.

The first significant mention of user value appears only later on in “Align your future with ours.” and the offerings “We invest with you”, “We invest for you”, and “We invest beyond you”, which communicate the bank’s core value more directly.

While the actual specifics of the offerings themselves are immaterial, the fact that they are offered is important. Because of this sequencing and the heavy use of whitespace, users must scroll too much before understanding the services, reducing the effectiveness of the homepage.
