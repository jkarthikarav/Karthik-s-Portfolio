Cinema Closet: Website Design

Designed 2024

Fashion, E-Commerce

For my INF2191 UI Design course, I was asked to design a website for a brand named Cinema Closet.

My instructions were simple:

A crowd-sourced platform where fans could discuss clothing worn by characters in films and TV shows.

Two Core Features: to allow for users search for clothing by browsing movies; and to Add items of clothing, accessories, or entire characters' "looks" to a collection
