Visa: Merchant Signage Website Redesign

Karthik Jambunathan

Published Dec 12, 2025

Context

Visa has a specialized e-commerce portal where businesses that accept Visa can order official, branded Point-of-Sale (POS) materials such as decals, stickers, and signs (including those for contactless and installment payments).

While the site is owned by Visa, the presence of the Staples' contact in the footer suggests that Staples is the third-party logistics and fulfillment partner responsible for managing the website and handling the shipping + customer service.

Existing Design

What did I observe?

Content-wise, it isn't at fault.

Accessibility. Yellow-Gold Buttons with White Labels? While the colors are from Visa's guidelines, Visa uses black on Yellow-Gold, not white.

Cannot Quick Add item to Cart. The User needs to go from Home > Merchant Signage (or View all and then scroll to item) > Click on item to open product page > Add to Cart.

My Redesign

In my redesign, I've simplified the hierarchy, reorganized the information, and worked to make existing content fit while following both VISA's brand guidelines and WCAG Accessibility guidelines (Both of which Staples, I assume manages the website, has failed to do). I also attempted the homepage in both of Canada's official languages, because I wanted to test how I could make the same design work for different languages.
