Meet a different kind of Designer

I take research all the way to the final build.

Usually research, design, and development are three different people. Something gets misread or left out every time work passes between them. I do all three myself so the thing I learned in research is still the thing shaping the build at the end.

I've worked in industries I knew nothing about going in: nonprofits, film production, university admissions, healthcare. Each time I had to learn enough to ask questions that made sense and recognize answers that mattered. I did it fast enough that the work was credible.

I also build things that keep working after I leave. The people who took over my projects didn't have to call me to figure out how anything worked. Some of them extended what I built into things I hadn't planned for.

Currently based in Toronto. Open to full-time roles.
