Meet a different kind of Designer

I take research all the way to the final build.

Usually research, design, and development are three different people. Something gets misread or left out every time work passes between them. I do all three myself so the thing I learned in research is still the thing shaping the build at the end.

I've worked in industries I knew nothing about going in: nonprofits, university admissions, healthcare. Each time I had to learn enough to ask questions that made sense and recognize answers that mattered. I did it fast enough that the work was credible.

I also build things that keep working after I leave. The people who took over my projects didn't have to call me to figure out how anything worked — my work is always scaleable and my documentation game's on point.

Currently based in Toronto. Open to full-time roles.

Key Skills

I use these skills on every project no matter what industry or who I'm working with.

Research & Strategy
I figure out what the real problem is before anything gets designed. I do that through interviews, competitor research, and watching how people actually work.
Design & Delivery
I take a project from the first conversation all the way, figuring out what goes where and making design calls that still make sense when the thing actually gets built.
Production & Development
Front-end code, CMS setup, and systems that the next person can actually use without calling me. If it breaks the moment I leave, I didn't finish the job.
Compliance & Quality
Everything I ship works for people with accessibility needs. I build that in from the start so it doesn't become a problem to fix at the end.

Academic Foundations

My Design Journey started at the age of 16.

I had written my final exams for high school when I was informed about a Post Graduate level-opportunity at Anna University, one of the top universities in India.

I worked towards a multi-disciplinary degree that focused on Media, Culture, Business, and Technology, during which I worked on a UX project for UNICEF and undertook a UX co-op at Caterpillar. Starting out as a freelance graphic designer and legal consultant, I eventually started my own business consulting firm in 2021.

In 2023, I joined and subsequently graduated in 2025 from the Information sciences program at the University of Toronto, Canada's top University, with a specialization in User Experience Design.
