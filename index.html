My Work for the University of Toronto

———————

Between 2023-2025, I was studying Information Sciences and UX Design at the University of Toronto. Having originally applied for a student marketing role at the ECE department (ECE at U of T), I presented my experience and asked to be trusted with the website. From Jun 2024 - Mar 2025, I did manage the WordPress environments for both their public website and intranet, making content updates.

But the bigger questions they had were: How might we enable the Department Website to recruit Prospective Students? and How might we integrate the Toronto Nanofabrication Centre into the ECE digital environment? and How could I help achieve this?

UX Research to WordPress Development • Nov 2024 - Mar 2025

End-to-End Digital Production to Drive Student Recruitment for ECE at U of T

Students were not navigating to core informational pages, and the Homepage lacked form AND function. I redesigned and rebuilt multiple core pages to address a missed recruitment opportunity by shifting to a student-focused hierarchy, giving them a functional and aesthetic facelift.

What Karthik Achieved

———————

  • Business Impact:
    Transformed for student recruitment by directing high-intent traffic to key pages.

  • User Impact:
    Reduced time-to-value through a clearer, student-focused content hierarchy.

The Toronto Nanofabrication Centre (TNFC) which operated under ECE at U of T, reopened in November 2023 after a renovation. Its website existed separately and I was asked to assess options for the TNFC website in November 2024. I submitted my proposal in Novermber, I was given the greenlight to proceed with the actual development in February 2025.

UX Research to WordPress Development • Nov 2024 - Mar 2025

Migrating a standalone website into a standardized environment (Toronto Nanofabrication Centre)

Information regarding all divisions inside ECE at U of T, including 11 Teaching labs and Research projects across 9 domains, existed on the ECE website. Execpt One. Only the Toronto Nanofabrication Centre (TNFC) did not. TNFC's standalone website did not communicate its ECE operations nor reflect U of T's brand. I rebuilt the bloated standalone website as a compact & specialized section inside of ECE at U of T's own digital environment.
