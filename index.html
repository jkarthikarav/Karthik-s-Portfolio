IA and Navigation Menu Redesign: Textile Museum of Canada

Redesigned the Information Architecture to improve content discoverability, accessibility, and user engagement

Overview

Redesigned the website’s information architecture to make key content easier to find, streamline navigation, and support ticketing, donations, and program discovery. Conducted card sorting, content audits, and tree testing to validate the structure. Designed mid- and high-fidelity wireframes for desktop and mobile, and proposed a phased implementation separating IA changes from navigation redesign to gather actionable insights.

Mobile App for Ticket Deals: UX/UI Research

From research to high-fidelity UI, designed a mobile-first platform that centralized discovery of discounted and free museum tickets.

Overview

Toronto is rich in cultural heritage, yet many residents miss out on museum experiences due to fragmented information about deals and free admission. Our project aimed to reduce this friction by centralizing ticket discovery through a mobile-first experience. I led user research, persona development, design prioritization, and the translation of insights into interactive Figma prototypes, iterating from low to high fidelity with feedback from peers and senior UX researchers.
