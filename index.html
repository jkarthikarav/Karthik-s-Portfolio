Consulting and Entrepreneurship

—

In 2021, I co-founded JKR Business Consultancy to take on problems that needed both a diagnosis and a delivery. The work has spanned industries, but what has stayed consistent is the depth each context required.

In healthcare, the problem was never the service itself; it was understanding how patients chose a clinic, how they became aware of closer locations, and how post-visit outreach brought them back. In industrial manufacturing, it was designing a sales workflow, then ensuring the financial documentation behind it was clean enough to hold up for accounting, tax compliance, and payment. In fintech SaaS, it meant building a testing tool, which required understanding the software it was testing and the customer trying to implement it. In K–12 e-learning, it was meeting WCAG requirements and raising the UI quality standard ahead of a funding round.

Outside of JKR, I've taken on independent projects where the same principle applies: own the problem, build the solution, stay until it works.

Digital Transformation: Building AI Into the Blueprint

Generative AI arrived in the creative industry with most studios left reacting to its disruption.
