Meet a different kind of Design

Most designers hand over aesthetic mockups. I do UX, Copy, and front-end.

I come from research, understanding people to mapping systems to finding the real problem underneath the stated one. But I also write the copy that goes in the interface and build the front-end that ships it. That end-to-end range means I spend less time in translation and more time solving.

I've worked across higher education, film and streaming, healthcare, and nonprofits. The problems are different. The approach is the same: find the real problem, build the right thing, make sure it lasts.
