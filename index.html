Featured Work

The University of Toronto's ECE Department needed someone who they could trust to help rebuild the full digital lifecycle. For the first 7 months, I had worked across the public website and intranet, handling content updates and WordPress/CMS management, building enough institutional knowledge to be trusted with something larger. In December 2024, that trust translated into a direct mandate: rebuild multiple core pages, deliver new page builds, and give one of Canada's most respected engineering departments a functional and aesthetic facelift.

UX Research to Development • Dec 2024 - Mar 2025

End-to-End Digital Production to Drive Student Recruitment for ECE at U of T

Students were ignoring the homepage rather than navigating through it; it was a missed recruitment opportunity. this included content updates and a full homepage redesign, shipped alongside multiple internal pages designed to convert the traffic it would receive.

  • Business Impact: Transformed for student recruitment by directing high-intent traffic to key pages.
  • User Impact: Reduced time-to-value through a clearer, student-focused content hierarchy.

Service Design • Digital Transformation • Creative Operations • 2022 - 2023

Digital Transformation: Building AI Into the Blueprint

Generative AI arrived in the creative industry with most studios left reacting to its disruption. Fix It In Post Studios chose to build services centered around generative AI, and I designed the responsible adoption framework that made that possible, validated through a pilot that turned an experimental workflow into a structured, billable service.

  • 30–40% revenue growth from a newly billable GenAI-assisted workflow.
  • ~2 weeks cut from production timelines.
  • Reduced client revision loops and eliminated AI anxiety across the team.
