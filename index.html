Meet a different kind of Designer

Usually research, design, and development are three different people. Something gets misread or left out every time work passes between them. I do all three myself so the thing I learned in research is still the thing shaping the build at the end.

I've worked in industries I knew nothing about going in. Each time I had to learn enough to ask questions that made sense and recognize answers that mattered.

The people who took over my projects didn't have to call me to ask how anything worked—my work's scalable and my documentation game's on point.

Currently based in Toronto. Open to full-time roles.

TORONTO NANOFABRICATION CENTRE (TNFC)

Integrating the discovery and onboarding experience of TNFC into its parent department's digital ecosystem.

As a direct consequence of my work, TNFC became a part of ECE at U of T's public-facing website rather than operating as a separate and outdated web property.

Stronger brand cohesion

TNFC looks and feels like ECE, not a separate organization.

Scalable structure

The pattern can be reused for future facilities that need a subsite.

Better user wayfinding

Users can find TNFC without leaving ECE's primary website.
