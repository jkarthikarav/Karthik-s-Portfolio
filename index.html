Introduction

This collection showcases digital systems designed to solve complex information challenges. The projects below represent my varied specialities: high-impact consultancy to institutional UX architecture, covering both UI/UX for Web and UX for Business Process Optimization.

Deep dives into UX with a focus on informational sensemaking and accessibility, including my web production work for the University of Toronto (ECE) and UX projects for creative industries.

Information Architecture • Institutional Web • Accessibility (AODA) • Shipped Jan 2025

ECE Homepage Redesign: Rebuilding Information Flow for Recruitment

Fragmented information flow was hindering student recruitment and departmental engagement. I led the restructuring in Figma and personally handled the WordPress implementation.

Business Impact: Solved the recruitment drop-off by ensuring high-value traffic was routed to the Program and Research pages.

User Impact: Reduced the time-to-value for students by reorganizing content into an accessible architecture.

B2C Mobile • Product Discovery • Market Research • Oct-Dec 2023

Ticket Discoverability App: Structuring Information Flow for Cultural Access

People struggled to find existing GLAM (Galleries, Libraries, Archives, and Museums) discounts in Toronto. Conducted product research, then prototyped a mobile solution focused on information flow and social sharing.

Projected Business Impact: Increases institutional revenue by converting interest into attendance through a centralized deal discovery.

Projected User Impact: Eliminates search tax for users by providing a single source for cultural access.
