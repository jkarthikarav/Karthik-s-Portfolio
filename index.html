Mobile App for Ticket Deals: End-to-End UX/UI Design

We designed a mobile-first platform that centralizes discovery of discounted and free museum tickets.

Overview

Toronto is rich in cultural heritage, yet many residents miss out on museum experiences due to fragmented information about deals. Our project reduced this friction by centralizing ticket discovery through a mobile-first experience. We conducted user research, persona creation, and low fidelity prototype design. Later, based on research insights, validation of low fidelity prototype, and feedback from peers and senior UX researchers, I iterated from low- to high-fidelity, building a design system and style guide adhering to Material Design guidelines.
