Bordier Website Homepage Redesign

Designed 2025

Private Banking Sector

View Current/Original Image

The hero section focuses on broad reputation claims like “The world’s leading Swiss private banker”. The follow-up paragraph about heritage and trust adds more narrative through “For 5 generations the Bordier family has been preserving and growing wealth…” but still does not explain the actual services. The real user value appears only later on in “Align your future with ours.” and the offerings “We invest with you”, “We invest for you”, and “We invest beyond you”, which communicate the bank’s core value more directly. While the actual specifics of the offerings themselves are immaterial, the fact that they are offered is important. Because of this sequencing and the heavy use of whitespace, users must scroll too much before understanding the services, reducing the effectiveness of the homepage.

In my redesign, I've reduced whitespace and reorganized the information to have a more focused narrative-feel.

Selenium.Dev Website Homepage Redesign

Designed 2025

Technology Sector

The page makes users work too hard to understand Selenium’s value. The homepage is dominated by a heavy wall of content. The bright green brand color is used everywhere as backgrounds, headings, links, and buttons across multiple surface colors, so nothing stands out.

In my redesign, I've made it so that the Product cards are easy to scan. I've kept the Selenium green, but used a darker shade for links and buttons for providing a visual distinction.
